Sandpainter

A falling-sand painting toy that runs in your browser.

26 elements to paint with. They burn, freeze, grow and explode.

How it is made

  • Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. No framework and no server.
  • Written from scratch as a remake of Artsology's Sandpainting Game v2.
  • 28 automatic checks keep the physics honest.
