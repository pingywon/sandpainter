Sandpainter

Pick an element on the left, drag on the canvas to paint it. Right-click or hold Shift to erase. The four spigots above the canvas pour on their own. Click a spigot's name to choose what it pours, and its bars to set how hard. Off stops it; click any bar to start it again.
